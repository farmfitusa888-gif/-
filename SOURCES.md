# Sources

Every URL consulted, 2026-08-27, grouped by the brief that uses it.

**Provenance classes**, as used throughout:

- **[vendor]** — read off the company's own page.
- **[review]** — reported by a third-party review, comparison or industry site,
  **not independently confirmed**.
- **[standard]** — a published technical or legal standard.
- **[assumption]** — mine, marked at the point of use.

## A warning about the [review] class

A large share of the pricing below comes from comparison and "best X software
2026" sites. **Many of these are operated by a competitor in the same category,
or are affiliate-monetised.** Two examples visible in this research: the
Yembo-vs-HomeSurvey.ai comparison is published by HomeSurvey.ai, and a PT EMR
pricing comparison naming WebPT, Prompt and Raintree is published by SPRY, a
competitor to all three.

**Treat every [review] figure as a lead to verify with the vendor, not a fact.**
Before acting on any of these numbers, get them from the vendor directly.
