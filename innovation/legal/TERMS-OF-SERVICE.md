## 2. What Countercite does

You give Countercite two things: an insurance denial letter, and the insurance
policy it refers to. Countercite reads both and reports the places where the
letter and the policy do not line up. For example, where the letter quotes part
of a provision and stops before an exception, or where it cites a provision that
does not appear in the policy you gave us.

**Every finding points at a page.** Each one names the document, the page number
and the exact words, in your own files, so you can open the page and check the
work yourself. That is the whole design. If a finding cannot be traced to a
page, Countercite does not make it.

Countercite can also assemble those findings into a draft letter. The draft
quotes your documents and asks the insurer to explain or reconsider. You edit
it, you approve it, and **you** send it, under your own name.

## 3. What Countercite is not

**We are not a public insurance adjuster.** We do not adjust claims. We do not

**We do not tell you whether you will win.** Countercite does not assess

**We do not file anything and we do not send anything.** There is no send
button. Countercite produces a file. Everything after that is your decision.

**What you tell us is not protected by attorney-client privilege**, because we
are not attorneys. Nothing you upload becomes confidential in the way it would
be if you gave it to a lawyer. Section 7 explains how we do protect it.

**Hiring a lawyer or a licensed public adjuster is a reasonable thing to do**,
and using Countercite is not a reason to skip it. In some situations it is
clearly the better choice. We are not in a position to tell you which situation
you are in.
