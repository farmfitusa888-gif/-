## Provenance classes

- **[standard]** — a published regulation, statute or government/regulator document.
- **[review]** — reported by a third party: trade press, news, industry body,
  vendor blog, comparison site. **Not independently confirmed.**
- **[derived]** — arithmetic on figures here; inputs named at the point of use.
- **[assumption]** — mine, marked at the point of use.

## What limits this research, stated plainly

**1. Direct page fetches are blocked.** The network egress proxy in this
environment refused every attempt to fetch a page directly — confirmed failures
include `reddit.com`. **All figures below therefore arrive through search-engine
summaries of the linked pages, not from reading the pages themselves.**

**2. Consequently there are no verbatim quotes from forums, and there will not
be.** You asked for "people complaining at volume" as a proof standard and I could
not reach that source class. Rather than paraphrase forum posts I have not read —
or invent representative ones — **the demand evidence here rests on published
surveys, government and industry statistics, and observable pricing.** That is a
real gap against your brief and it is named here rather than glossed.

**3. Several sources are commercially interested in the number they report.**
Flagged individually below. Public adjuster fee ranges come largely from public
adjusting firms; night nurse rates from agencies selling night nurses; AAA's
distrust statistic is published alongside AAA's own repair network.

**4. No competitor's pricing was read from the vendor's own page.** Same proxy
limitation. Verify before acting on any price.
