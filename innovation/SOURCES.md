# Sources

Every search-derived claim in this project, grouped by the brief that uses it.
Researched **2026-08-28**.

## Provenance classes

- **[vendor]** — read off the company's own page. **There are none in this
  document. See the warning below.**
- **[review]** — reported by a third-party review, comparison, trade or news
  site, **not independently confirmed**.
- **[standard]** — a published technical standard or regulation.
- **[derived]** — arithmetic on figures here; inputs named at the point of use.
- **[assumption]** — mine, marked at the point of use.

## Two warnings that change how much you should trust this

**1. No vendor page was read directly.** The network egress proxy in this
environment blocked every attempted direct fetch (confirmed failures:
`sagereport.com/pricing/`, `www.mentalyc.com`). Every price below therefore
reaches this document through a *search engine's summary of a third-party page*
— two removes from the vendor. **Nothing here is [vendor] class. Every price is
[review].**

**2. Comparison sites are usually run by a competitor or an affiliate.**
Directly visible in this research: the Upheal-vs-Mentalyc comparison is
published by **Mentalyc**; multiple "best software 2026" pages are
affiliate-monetised; `transcribelegal.ai` and `matterscribe.com` publish court
reporter pricing while selling AI transcription in competition with it.

> **Verify every price by phoning the vendor before making any decision on it.**
