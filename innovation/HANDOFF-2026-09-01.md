# Handoff and close, 1 September 2026

The project is stopped. Not abandoned mid-thought: stopped on a finding, after
the numbers were checked from four directions and did not support the goal.

This file supersedes the earlier version written the same day, which stopped at
the entity question and did not yet have the revenue research.

**Total spent: nothing.** No domain, no entity, no subscription, no
infrastructure, no advertising. That was deliberate from the first day and it is
why stopping here costs only time.

---

## 1. Why it stopped

The owner set seven constraints (`CONSTRAINTS.md`). Each is individually
reasonable. Together they describe a business that pays roughly **$2,000 to
$8,000 a month after two to three years**, against a stated goal of $20,000 to
$50,000.

Four independent research passes converged on that, from different angles:

- `14-NO-CUSTOMER-STRUCTURES.md` did the general arithmetic and found the target
  off by about 4x.
- `15-MERCHANT-OF-RECORD.md` and `17-MOR-FOR-INDIVIDUALS.md` found that
  platforms which look like a shield are not one. Apple's own agreement makes
  the developer the principal, "solely responsible for any and all claims and
  liabilities". Paddle's own words keep the customer relationship with the
  seller.
- `18-AD-AND-AFFILIATE-RATES.md` priced the last candidate and came in at $8 per
  thousand sessions rather than the $25 assumed, because a lookup tool gets one
  or two pages a visit where article sites get three or four.

The constraint doing the damage was **not** the one that triggered the search.
Four separate passes found that essentially nothing requires a business entity;
individuals are accepted everywhere that matters. What binds is **no customer
plus no outreach together**, which leaves a recommendation algorithm as the only
possible source of demand and makes any such business a tenancy.

That is the whole finding. It is worth more than any of the code.

## 2. What was actually built, and what it is worth

| | |
|---|---|
| Tests | 99 across five suites, plus two that drive a real browser |
| Sites | 3, generated from one engine: Backpay 12 pages, Countercite 20, Flood Facts 7 |
| Research notes | 18, every claim labelled by how well it was verified |
| Legal drafts | 6, usable as starting points |
| Cost | $0 |

**Reusable regardless of what comes next:**

- `platform/` — a static site generator driving any number of sites from JSON,
  with a validator that fails the build on dead links, duplicate titles, missing
  canonicals and unparseable schema. It emits roughly 250 to 300 metadata
  signals a page against a competitor baseline of 21.
- `engines/ca-wage/` — a complete California wage-and-hour engine, 38 tests
  across the single-week and bulk paths. Correct as far as it has been tested,
  and it refuses four categories of input it cannot model rather than guessing.
- `engines/contradiction/` — a document contradiction finder whose types make
  "every finding cites its source" a property of the code rather than a promise,
  plus a 30-rule output filter that blocks legal-advice phrasing.
- The pipeline pattern, proven four times: a public dataset nobody can use, a
  fetcher written against the documented interface, a parser tested against a
  fixture with known parameters, and honest suppression of anything too thin to
  report.

**The habit worth keeping, independent of the code:** every number in this
repository is labelled with how well it was checked. Where a figure could not be
verified it says so, and where a research pass could not reach a primary source
it says that at the top rather than in a footnote. Several conclusions here
reversed earlier ones, and they reversed because the labels made it obvious
which claims were soft.

## 3. The things that turned out to be true, and are worth carrying anywhere

1. **An entity is not the barrier.** Sole proprietors are accepted by every ad
   network, every app store, and seven of eight merchant-of-record services. The
   advice to form one is generic business advice, not a signal about any
   particular idea.

2. **Merchant of record moves the money, not the relationship.** Apple, Google
   Play and Paddle all leave the end-user relationship with the seller. Anyone
   planning around the opposite is planning around something that is not true.

3. **Spotting a gap is no longer an edge.** Five of twelve rejected opportunities
   died because somebody had already built them, and those builds look like
   one-person AI projects from the last eighteen months. What survives is work
   that is annoying at a scale where AI genuinely helps.

4. **Specificity is not protection from AI Overviews.** Long queries trigger them
   roughly five times as often as short ones. The thing protecting financial
   content today is a Google policy throttle, not a property of the subject.

5. **How the fee is computed matters more than who pays it.** Flat and
   outcome-independent is what keeps a business out of regulated territory.
   Contingent fees are what convert software into a licensed activity.

## 4. If any of it is ever picked up again

Read in this order, and stop early if the first one settles it:

1. `CONSTRAINTS.md` — because the constraints are the finding, not the ideas.
2. `research/14-NO-CUSTOMER-STRUCTURES.md` verdict box and
   `research/18-AD-AND-AFFILIATE-RATES.md` verdict box. Between them they
   contain the whole commercial argument.
3. `research/11-LIABILITY-SCREEN.md` if the question is which subject matter is
   safe rather than which idea is good.
4. `node tools/status.mjs` for live counts rather than the numbers above, which
   age.

The one number that was never established and would change the ad-supported
case: county-level search volume. It sits behind a keyword tool login this
environment cannot reach, and it is about an hour of work on an ordinary
computer.

## 5. What was wrong along the way, kept on the record

Left in deliberately, because a project that only records its successes teaches
nothing on a second reading.

- The first eight ideas were rejected wholesale, correctly. They deferred
  validation to the reader.
- Fee figures in the insurance idea were unlawful as modelled; two states cap at
  10% and the model used 15%.
- The chosen licensing structure turned out not to exist in either launch state.
- A claim that every helpline is shut at night was wrong; the Alzheimer's
  Association runs a free 24/7 line.
- The house style document failed its own em-dash rule on first draft.
- Work was handed back to the owner three times that should have been researched
  here, which produced `WHO-DOES-WHAT.md`.
- The Flood Facts README claimed a county-level answer survives AI Overviews.
  The revenue research refuted it and the correction sits where the claim was.
- An FAQ schema bug meant all three sites rendered their FAQs visibly and
  emitted no structured data at all. Found by checking a number rather than
  trusting it.

---

## 6. The honest summary

The engineering was sound. The research was careful and repeatedly corrected
itself. The constraint set was coherent and the owner held it consistently.

The three did not fit together, and finding that out cost nothing but time.
