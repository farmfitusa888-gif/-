# Considered and cut

Recorded so a gap is never mistaken for an oversight — and because two of these
were written up as ideas before the competitive check killed them. **That check is
the step I skipped in the previous version of this project**, and it is why this
list exists.

---

## Cut because someone well-funded is already there

### Estate and probate settlement — cut, and it hurt

The pain evidence is extraordinary. Executors spend an average of **570 hours**
settling an estate; most spend **500–700**. The average person dealing with
probate spends **a year of their life, $14,000, and 12 hours a week** [review].
Median legal fees for an estate under $500,000 run about **$4,000**; flat-fee
probate work is **$3,000–7,000** [review].

**I wrote this up as the strongest idea in the set. Then I checked the market.**

| Company | Position | Class |
|---|---|---|
| **Alix** | **$30.65M raised**, incl. $20M Series A led by Acrew with **Charles Schwab** and **Edward Jones Ventures** participating. Dedicated specialist coordinating 100+ tasks | [review] |
| **Empathy** | App + human care managers + AI assistant "Lila"; **distributed free through life insurance carriers**, so families pay nothing | [review] |
| **Atticus** | DIY executor software, **$175–499** | [review] |
| Elayne, Sunset, SwiftProbate | Account closing, asset discovery, executor tooling | [review] |

Every entry point is taken: premium-with-a-human, free-via-insurer, and cheap DIY.
Empathy's model is the killer — **you cannot beat free**, and a distribution deal
with life insurers is not a thing a solo operator wins.

### Property tax appeals

**30–60% of homes are over-assessed** in a given year; average savings
**$1,000–3,000**; appeal success rates **40–60%**; **64%** of homeowners were
surprised or shocked by their 2026 bill [review]. Textbook asymmetry.

But Ownwell (~25% contingency), TaxDrop, AppealDesk ($49 flat), TaxAppealUSA and
property tax attorneys already cover every price point [review]. The category
found its answer before we got there.

### Medical bill negotiation

**80% of medical bills contain at least minor errors**; the average hospital bill
over $10,000 carries about **$1,300** in overcharges; **100 million Americans** owe
**$220 billion** in medical debt; roughly **40%** who challenge a bill get a
reduction [review].

Cut anyway: CareRoute (18–25% of savings, capped at $1,000), RecoverKit and others
are already AI-native here [review], and **Counterforce Health** is doing
AI-assisted health insurance denial appeals [review]. Note the asymmetry with idea
2 — the *health* insurance appeal space is contested; the *property* insurance
appeal space is where the research explicitly says the funding isn't going.

### Clinical dementia care navigation

Distinct from idea 6, and taken: **Rippl ($23M Series A)**, **Craniometrix
($7.5M)**, **SafelyYou ($114.3M)**, Harmonic Health, and Medicare's GUIDE
programme as the reimbursement channel [review]. Idea 6 survives only because it
attacks the consumer 2am moment these do not serve.

### Smart water leak detection

The need is real — average water damage claim **$15,400**, **23%** of all
homeowner claims, **1 in 60** homes annually [review]. But the hardware is
commoditised and the insurers have already taken the channel: Chubb offers **8%**
off, credits generally run **3–10%**, and Farmers, Liberty Mutual, USAA, Amica and
Mercury all run device discount programmes [review]. There is no room between the
device makers and the carriers subsidising them.

**This was my only hardware candidate with real evidence. I did not replace it
with a weaker one to fill a slot.** Seven researched ideas beat eight with a
manufactured eighth.

## Cut on structure rather than competition

| Candidate | Why |
|---|---|
| **Child protective services documentation** | The pain evidence is the strongest of anything found across both versions of this project — **15%** of the week in front of families, **~400 forms and ~2,500 pages per case**, **30–40%** turnover, **$54,000** per departing caseworker in Texas [review]. It fails on buyer: a state procurement office, 12–24 month cycle. Reachable only through private in-home providers, which is a much smaller and unestablished population. |
| **Consumer subscription cancelling / bill audit** | Rocket Money and others own it; low willingness to pay; one-time value. |
| **General AI receptionist for small business** | Genuinely large demand, and genuinely crowded — dozens of funded entrants. No angle found that a solo operator holds for long. |
| **Anything requiring a two-sided marketplace** | Allowed by the brief, but a cold-start problem is incompatible with a 6–12 month revenue target for a solo operator. Ruled out on timeline, not on merit. |

## The pattern in the cuts

Five of the six competitive cuts share a shape: **a large, well-evidenced consumer
pain where a funded company has already secured a distribution channel** — life
insurers, Medicare GUIDE, property carriers, tax-appeal SEO.

That is the real lesson for choosing among what survived. In this category the
product is rarely the hard part; **the channel is.** The surviving ideas were kept
disproportionately because they have a channel nobody has locked: employment law
firms and unions (1), the licensed-adjuster relationship (2 and 5), hospital and
health-plan economics (3), professional associations (5), and support groups (6).
